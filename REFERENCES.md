# References and source map

## AI applications linked in the proposal

- Z. Li, S. Sajadmanesh, J. Li, and L. Lyu. “StelLA: Subspace Learning in Low-rank Adaptation using Stiefel Manifold.” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 38*, 2025. [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/6cb0c6e7d50d5d65613f0456ca85e2db-Abstract-Conference.html). Used to motivate explicit subspace learning with Stiefel-constrained factors.
- F. Bian, J. Zheng, Z. Liu, J. Luo, and J.-F. Cai. “Finding Low-Rank Matrix Weights in DNNs via Riemannian Optimization: RAdaGrad and RAdamW.” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 38*, 2025. [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/5679173c400b332796426e443ab5ea0d-Abstract-Conference.html). Used to contrast optimizing a fixed-rank matrix object with optimizing redundant factor coordinates.
- Y. Lin, C. Fu, Z. Krueger, H. Yu, M. Nakata, J. Xie, E. Kucukbenli, X. Qian, and S. Ji. “Tensor Decomposition Networks for Fast Machine Learning Interatomic Potential Computations.” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 38*, 2025. [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/7fe3f83c15c1c96daf4689d358c9cadf-Abstract-Conference.html). Used as an architectural example of replacing expensive tensor-product operators with CP structure.
- A. Ghorbani, J. Wexler, J. Y. Zou, and B. Kim. “Towards Automatic Concept-based Explanations.” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 32*, 2019. [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/77d2afcb31f6493e350fca61764efb9a-Abstract.html). Introduces ACE and motivates the discovery and validation of dataset-level, human-understandable candidate concepts.
- T. Fel, A. Picard, L. Béthune, T. Boissin, D. Vigouroux, J. Colin, R. Cadène, and T. Serre. “CRAFT: Concept Recursive Activation FacTorization for Explainability.” *CVPR*, 2023, 2711-2721. [CVF Open Access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Fel_CRAFT_Concept_Recursive_Activation_FacTorization_for_Explainability_CVPR_2023_paper.html). Lab 4 follows the paper's crop-activation NMF, Concept Activation Vector, recursive-layer, and Sobol-importance ideas with synthetic teaching data. Its matrix-versus-tensor section is explicitly identified as a TensorKitchen extension question, not part of CRAFT.
- A. Templeton et al. “Scaling Monosemanticity: Extracting Interpretable Features from Claude 3 Sonnet.” Anthropic research publication, 2024; arXiv:2605.29358 version, 2026. The dual date distinguishes the original 2024 publication from the later arXiv record used in the glossary.

## Mathematical and software foundations

- P. Breiding and N. Vannieuwenhoven. “A Riemannian Trust Region Method for the Canonical Tensor Rank Approximation Problem.” *SIAM Journal on Optimization* 28(3), 2018, 2435-2465. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/17M114618X). Provides the product-of-Segre viewpoint and geometric motivation for canonical tensor rank approximation.
- T. G. Kolda and B. W. Bader. “Tensor Decompositions and Applications.” *SIAM Review* 51(3), 2009, 455-500. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/07070111X). General reference for tensor notation, CP, Tucker, and multilinear products.
- J. Mairal, F. Bach, J. Ponce, and G. Sapiro. “Online Dictionary Learning for Sparse Coding.” *Proceedings of the 26th International Conference on Machine Learning*, 2009, 689-696. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/1553374.1553463). Provides the dictionary-learning perspective used to introduce learned directions and their coefficients in Lab 4.
- D. D. Lee and H. S. Seung. “Learning the Parts of Objects by Non-negative Matrix Factorization.” *Nature* 401, 1999, 788-791. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/44565). Establishes the additive, nonnegative representation that motivates the NMF explanation in Lab 4.
- TensorKitchen.jl. [Software repository](https://github.com/TensorKitchen/TensorKitchen.jl). The notebooks declare compatibility with TensorKitchen ~0.2.0 in Project.toml.
